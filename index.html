About Hudson Valley Auto Interiors

For over 35 years, Hudson Valley Auto Interiors has been the trusted name in automotive upholstery restoration. We specialize in the fabrication and installation of convertible tops and premium interiors for vintage, classic, and high-end automobiles.

Our digital archive represents decades of meticulous work, preserving the exact specifications and patterns of classic car interiors. Each pattern in our collection has been carefully measured, documented, and verified to ensure authenticity.

Whether you're a professional installer or a passionate enthusiast, our archive provides you with the precision patterns and expert support you need to restore your classic automobile to its original glory.
